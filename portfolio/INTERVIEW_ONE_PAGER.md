# Interview One-Pager — Marketing Analytics Decision Lab

**Problem:** Choose the analytical method that best matches a marketing decision rather than forcing every question into one framework.

**Methods:** inference, Bass diffusion, clustering, factorial ANOVA, logistic regression, factor analysis/reliability, conjoint/choice modeling, and LTV.

**Business value:** connects statistical output to segmentation, product/package design, adoption, campaign decisions, preference tradeoffs, and retention economics.

**Publication choice:** original Excel/SPSS datasets are not redistributed; public documentation distinguishes source-derived course results from future portfolio reproductions.
