# Conjoint and LTV Evidence

One conjoint exercise estimated importance of price at 36.9%, calories 33.2%, brand 18.1%, and packaging 11.7%. A sports-package exercise placed greatest importance on ticket location (39.5%) and ticket price (37.6%), supporting a recommended configuration with projected revenue around $3.4M versus $2.6M for an alternative.

The LTV exercise connected retention to customer economics: a baseline LTV of 2.5 at 75% retention required approximately 87.5% retention to double to 5 under the exercise assumptions.
